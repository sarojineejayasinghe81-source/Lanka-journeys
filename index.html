Good to know before you go

Best time to visit

Sri Lanka has two monsoons, so the best coast depends on the month. December to March suits the west and south coasts and the hill country. April to September suits the east coast. Use the month finder at the top of this page.

Visa and entry

Most visitors need an electronic travel authorisation before arriving. Rules and fees change, so check the official Sri Lanka government site before you fly.

Money and mobile data

The currency is the Sri Lankan rupee (LKR). Cards work in most hotels and larger restaurants. Carry cash for small towns. Tourist SIM cards are sold at the airport.

Temple etiquette

Cover your shoulders and knees, and remove your shoes and hat before entering. Never pose with your back to a Buddha statue.

Food to try

Rice and curry, hoppers, kottu roti, fresh seafood on the coast and Ceylon tea.
