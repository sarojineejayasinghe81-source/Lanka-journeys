Kandy

Sacred temple and hill capital

Kandy is the last royal capital of Sri Lanka and the start of the famous hill-country train line.

Best time to go

Pleasant most of the year. The Esala Perahera festival is in July or August.

How to get there

About 3 hours from Colombo by road or train.

Things to do in Kandy

  • Visit the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic
  • Stroll Peradeniya Royal Botanical Gardens
  • Watch a traditional Kandyan dance show in the evening
  • Walk around Kandy Lake and the old town
