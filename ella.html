Ella

Tea hills, trains and viewpoints

Ella is a small hill town with cafes, waterfalls and some of the best walks in the country.

Best time to go

Dry and clear from January to March. Mornings give the best views.

How to get there

The Kandy to Ella train takes about 6 to 7 hours. Reserve seats early. By car it is about 6 hours from Colombo.

Things to do in Ella

  • Ride the Kandy to Ella train through the tea country
  • Walk to the Nine Arches Bridge to see a train cross
  • Hike Little Adam's Peak, an easy walk with wide views
  • Visit a tea factory and taste fresh Ceylon tea
