Mirissa

Beaches, surf and whale watching

Mirissa is a relaxed south-coast beach town and the main base for blue whale watching.

Best time to go

Best from December to April. Whale watching runs roughly November to April.

How to get there

About 3.5 hours from Colombo airport by expressway and car.

Things to do in Mirissa

  • Join an early morning whale and dolphin boat trip
  • Watch sunset at Coconut Tree Hill
  • Learn to surf at the gentle Weligama bay nearby
  • Eat fresh grilled seafood on the beach
