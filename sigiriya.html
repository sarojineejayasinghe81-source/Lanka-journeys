Sigiriya

Rock fortress and sunrise climbs

Sigiriya is a 200 m granite rock topped with the ruins of a royal palace. It is the highlight of Sri Lanka's cultural triangle.

Best time to go

Jan to Sep is driest. Climb at opening time (about 7:00) before the heat.

How to get there

About 4 hours by car from Colombo airport, or 1.5 hours from Kandy.

Things to do in Sigiriya

  • Climb Sigiriya Rock, the 5th-century fortress with the mirror wall and frescoes
  • Watch sunrise from Pidurangala Rock for the best view of Sigiriya itself
  • Visit the Dambulla cave temples, about 30 minutes away
  • Take a village jeep safari to Minneriya or Kaudulla for elephants
